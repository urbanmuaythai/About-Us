OUR ORIGIN
STORY.

Told by founder Richard Walsh

Age 9

A rough road

At the age of 9 I was a victim of a school yard assault that left me feeling vulnerable and unsure how to respond. My brother came to my defense using extreme violence on the perpetrator. Thinking this was the answer, it led me down a rough road over the coming years.

"Hit first before being hit."

Then being a teenager and not knowing where I fit in society was a very scary time. I responded with mischief, fuelled by violence and alcohol.

Finding my footing

Muaythai saved my life

It was at this time I was fortunate enough to find Muaythai, and its culture changed and saved my life. Here I craved more knowledge, and after an unsuccessful start to my Muaythai fighting career with an inexperienced coach, I found myself being a coach — not only to myself, but to others in my hometown.

A career of over 30 fights saw me fall in love with the sport and gave me the desire to give others what I didn't have: an experienced coach who could see how to help me improve and succeed. An eye for ring craft has since become a core part of who I am.

Fighter to trainer

From the ring to the world stage

Muaythai became life, and as the years went by I changed from fighter to trainer, training some of Australia's best Muaythai fighters of the time to multiple world championship status. I was fortunate to travel the world with them, continuing to learn and improve from other world-class trainers — and that has led me to be the coach I am today.

2001

Meeting Melissa

I met Melissa, who is now my wife, in 2001, where we forged an unbreakable friendship and training partnership that saw her go on to win 23 of 25 fights, and win the Amateur World Title.

We enjoyed being part of the community, and we were part of the lifestyle that is Muay Thai — the camaraderie, the great memories, being part of a fight team that was successful on every level, all whilst feeling on top of the world with health and fitness. We wanted to share this addictive sport with everyone, and be part of each fighter's journey so they too could experience what we had.

This is what we call the "Magic of Muaythai".

2010

Opening our doors

With the desire to create our own community, we opened Urban Fight Gym in 2010, later changing the name to Urban Muaythai.

Today

Why we do this

We want everyone to be happy and feel like they belong from day one. We want to provide a community where new lifelong friendships are made — a place where you're able to become the best version of you, both physically and mentally. Whether a person competes in Muaythai or not is irrelevant, as each person can feel successful on many other levels, whether that's through our unique grading system, the Urban Warrior Challenge, or just regular training.

One thing is for sure, and we see it so many times over within our gym: the transformation it makes to everyday people's lives. We see people, from kids to adults, come in lacking confidence and communication skills, and grow rapidly from a system that focuses on inclusion, teamwork, camaraderie and mental resilience.

Our vision

To empower people to live a better life through the Magic of Muaythai.
